WITNESS STATEMENT                                              PAGE 1 OF 2

Name:        Ruth Okoye
Position:    Night security officer, gate booth
Shift:       14 March 18:00 to 15 March 07:00
Taken:       15 March, 07:10, at the gate booth

I came on duty at 18:00 and took the handover from the day officer. The
handover book noted two booked visits for the night: the courier drop at the
west loading bay at 23:30, and nothing else. Day staff were still leaving
until about 19:00. Dr Arden was working late in the west wing seed labs, as he
often does. I did not see him leave. I was at the booth window most of the
evening and the main door is not visible from my chair.

Mara Quill arrived at about 21:50 and waved at the booth on her way in, as
usual. She started her shift at 22:00.

ROUND 22:00. I left the booth at 22:00. East checkpoint at 22:02, all east
doors tried and locked: potting house 1, potting house 2, the boiler room,
the staff kitchen. Prep room light on, Mara at her bench. West corridor at
22:05, seed labs W1 and W2 locked, lights off. Vault V2 door closed, panel
green. West checkpoint at 22:07. Loading bay door closed and locked, spare
hi-vis on its hook. Back in the booth at 22:11.

At about 23:35 the courier's van arrived and drove round to the west loading
bay. I did not open the bay for him. I assumed Mara had let him in, as she
usually meets the courier. At about 23:45 I saw a figure in a hi-vis jacket
at the bay, which I took to be the courier unloading. The van was still at
the bay when I went out on my midnight round.

ROUND 00:00. Left the booth at 00:00. East checkpoint at 00:02, east doors
locked, kitchen light off. Prep room light on, Mara not at her bench, I
believe she was in the potting houses. West corridor at 00:05, labs locked,
vault door closed, panel green. West checkpoint at 00:06. Through the bay
window I could see the courier's van with its cab light on. I did not speak
to the driver. Back in the booth at 00:12. The van left at about 00:15.

Name:        Ruth Okoye

Between midnight and two the site was quiet. It began to rain at about
01:40. Mara came by the booth at some point in the night to say hello.

ROUND 02:00. I did my round at 02:00. Everything was locked and in order and
I saw nothing unusual.

The rest of the night was quiet. I logged the rain and the outside
temperature at 03:00 as required.

ROUND 04:00. Left the booth at 04:00. East checkpoint at 04:02, east doors
locked, the floor of potting house 2 wet from irrigation. West corridor at
04:05, labs locked, vault door closed, panel green. West checkpoint at 04:06.
Loading bay door closed and locked. I noticed the spare hi-vis was not on its
hook but on the bench by the door, folded. I hung it back up. Back in the
booth at 04:11.

At 05:52 Mara rang the booth from her phone and said the V2 freezer rack for
AL-07 was empty. She sounded upset. I went to the west corridor at 06:01 and
tried the vault door at 06:03 but my card does not open it. I rang the
director at 05:58, before I went over, and she arrived at about 06:38 and
opened the vault.

At 06:20, while waiting, I went to the staff kitchen to put the kettle on and
saw a receipt on top of the rubbish in the bin. It had the name of a lab
supply shop on it, so I bagged it with a clean freezer bag and wrote the time
on the bag. I did not read it closely. I handed it to the director.

I did not see Dr Arden, the courier, or anyone else in the building after
midnight apart from Mara. I have read this statement and it is true.

Signed:  R. Okoye                                     Time:  07:10
